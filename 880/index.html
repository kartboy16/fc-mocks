#880 Bulk upload social posts by CSV (like AFA)

Recommend A — an Upload CSV button on Social Media → Your posts, for the current advisor only. After a preview, valid rows are scheduled and flagged rows are skipped, with an accurate count. Do not add AFA’s “For all advisors / For [advisor] only” choice in this slice: FC Social Media is already advisor-scoped, and a firm-wide fan-out of the same calendar is a different, higher-risk admin tool. B is the same scope, but Schedule stays off until every flagged row is fixed or removed. C adds that all-advisors choice for admins, with a confirmation.
