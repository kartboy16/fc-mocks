Step 3 of 4 · Install

Install

  1. Unzip it and upload the insights folder to your website's main folder.
  2. Open example.com/insights once. Your posts should appear inside your own site's header and footer.

Not sure how? Send this page to whoever looks after your website.

Developer details
  • Requirements: PHP 7.4 or later, cURL or allow_url_fopen, and a writable insights/cache/ folder.
  • List: GET https://financialcontent.ca/api/posts/abc123 (published posts, with slug and updatedAt).
  • Post: GET https://financialcontent.ca/api/post/<post-id>?advisor=abc123. It returns 404 for anything not in the advisor's published set.
  • Routes: /insights (list) and /insights/<slug> (post), rendered on the server. The zip includes an .htaccess rewrite; for nginx, add try_files $uri /insights/index.php?$args;.
  • Header and footer: edit insights/config.php to point HEADER_FILE and FOOTER_FILE at your site's own header and footer (or paste the HTML into header.html and footer.html).
  • SEO: each page sets <title>, meta description, a canonical URL on your domain, Open Graph tags and Article JSON-LD.
  • Cache: 15 minutes. If FinancialContent can't be reached, the last good copy is shown (stale fallback). If the subscription ends, the API stops returning posts and the cache is cleared.
  • Status: the renderer sends User-Agent: FC-Insights-PHP/1.0 and your site address, so we can show "last fetched" here.
