#654 Multiple email header & footer templates

Advisors save more than one header & footer (for example, one co-branded with Partner Firm and one with Your Firm Name only) and pick one for each email, in FTT Mail and Mailchimp. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev hold until Alex picks.

Recommend A: a template list in Email Settings and a "Header & footer" drop-down with live preview in compose. It's the smallest change for advisors and the same for FTT Mail and Mailchimp. About 6.5 to 9 days.

Mocks (each has a Desktop / Phone toggle)

  • Today (reference): one header and footer for every email. Edit in compose changes it everywhere.
  • A. Template list + picker (recommended): templates · compose (FTT Mail) · compose (Mailchimp) · campaigns list. Try Delete on "Co-branded" (reassign scheduled emails) or on the default (pick a new one), and the "First visit (migrated)" state.
  • B. Gallery in compose: pick by thumbnail, and editing asks "Save as new / Update / Only this email". About 7 to 9.5 days.
  • C. Separate headers and footers: mix and match, with a mismatch warning. About 8.5 to 11 days.
  • Build contract (.md): screens, states, migration, scheduled/sent rules, scope, mobile, backend, estimates and questions.

Deleting a template: sent emails keep what they were sent with (we save a copy at send). Scheduled FTT Mail emails must be moved to another template first. Mailchimp campaigns already in Mailchimp keep their copy. The partner disclosure text is a placeholder that needs compliance review.
