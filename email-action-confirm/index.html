Email action links: confirm pages

Mail security scanners open every link in an email. Today our Approve, Reject and Unsubscribe links act as soon as they're opened, so a scanner approved a campaign seconds after the email went out. With these pages, opening a link changes nothing. Only a button click does. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev hold until Alex picks.

Recommend A: a full confirm page (what you're approving, with Approve and Don't approve buttons). The click sends a POST with a signed, single-use, expiring token, and we record who approved, their IP and browser. About 5 to 7.25 days.

Mocks (each has a Desktop / Phone toggle and state buttons)

  • Today (reference): what happened, and today's pages.
  • A. Campaign approval: confirm · approved · already · declined · expired · error
  • A. Social post approve/reject: opened from the Reject link, with the same states.
  • A. Unsubscribe (client-facing, advisor branding only): done + undo · already · resubscribed
  • Slack #email_campaign_approved lines: approver, IP, browser, method, plus a "scanner open, no action" line.
  • B. Lighter confirm: title and one button. Same safety, less detail, about 4 to 6 days.
  • Build contract (.md): routes today, states, build note (POST, tokens, RFC 8058 one-click), estimate and open questions.

Example data only. Legal and compliance wording (unsubscribe footer, expiry rules) is a placeholder.
