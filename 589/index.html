#589 FTT Mail Subscribers: choose which columns to show

Includes fields imported from Mailchimp by #585 (phone, birthday, address and custom fields). Designer-first mock. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev hold until Alex picks.

Approved direction (Oct 19): today's columns stay the default. A Columns control on the toolbar lists every standard and custom field, with imported fields labelled. The choice is saved per advisor. The contact page always shows every field. Optional one-time hint after an import adds new fields.

Recommend A, the dropdown checklist. It's one click to open and one tick per field, it looks and works like the Filters menu advisors already use, and it's the smallest build. Reorder (B) can be added later without changing what's saved.

Each direction has a "Try a state" bar: default (today), choosing columns, the one-time hint after an import, many custom fields, no imported fields yet, no subscribers, and phone.
