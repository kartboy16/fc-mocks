Provisional build contract · Markdown

Problem: Infographics today are Canva JPEGs uploaded into FC, then stamped with logo/contact. There is no real per-advisor colour or font theming on the finished asset. Firms that keep FC after moving their agency site still want branded social/email graphics without another design tool login.
North star update (#288): These clicky-builder mocks are kept for contrast only. The new happy path is zero-click AI generate — see fc-mocks/288/ (recommend Direction A). Soft Dev hold · not agent-ready.
Architecture recommendation (30-second read)
  • Builder lives in-house in FC (content-library Meteor/React) as a first-class Create Infographic flow next to library / email.
  • Brand kit already lives in Settings & Branding — publish lands in the library; insert into email via existing Insert infographic.
  • Template-driven generate (HTML/SVG layouts → PNG). Do not recolour finished Canva JPEGs.
  • A separate design tool is wrong for this ICP: another login, no brand sync, harder compliance/review.
  • Canva stays an optional escape hatch for one-offs — not the default path.

Soft Dev confirms feasibility later. This mock is the design recommendation first.

Non-goals (v1)
  • Full Canva competitor / freeform design canvas
  • White-label the whole FC app
  • Recolouring finished JPEG uploads as the primary path
Open product questions — do not invent locked answers Resolve before agent-ready
  1. Brand colours/fonts: new fields under Settings & Branding, or only inside the builder?
  2. Who authors templates — FC admin only, or firm-level custom templates later?
  3. Output sizes: square vs story vs email widths?
  4. Does the generated PNG go through compliance approval like other library posts?
Track: Designer-first recommendation. Do not mark agent-ready; do not wake Software developer. Scout note: today’s Settings & Branding has logo/contact; colours/fonts are NEW (no hex/font theme found).
