#599 Weekly picks inside Manage Advisor Posts

Each managed advisor's weekly recommended post now lives inside Manage Advisor Posts (MAP), next to Past, Upcoming and Suggested. Admins schedule it with MAP's own Publish & share dialog and cancel it with MAP's own cancel. This is a designer-first mock with generic sample data. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev UI hold until Alex picks.

Decision (Oct 21): Alex changed direction. Build #599 into MAP instead of a separate review screen. This replaces 599-rotation-review, which stays online for reference.

Rules shown on every screen: nothing is scheduled until you click Schedule and confirm · drafts never publish on their own · picks are built every Monday at 5:00 AM PT · posts that need an update are skipped, with the reason shown.

Recommend A, "Recommended this week" inside each MAP row. The pick sits right where admins already schedule from. It reuses the Suggested card, the schedule dialog and the cancel dialog, and adds no route or tab. It's the smallest build: about 2–2.5 UI days plus 1.5–2 backend days. For the cross-advisor view, I recommend A's light filter strip (Due, Scheduled, Skipped, Needs update) and not a separate page.

Options

  • A. "Recommended this week" block in each row's Posts cell (recommended): between Upcoming and Suggested, with this month's topic and the last 3 months beside it. A filter strip above the table is the light overview. No bulk. UI 2–2.5 days, backend 1.5–2.
  • B. "Weekly picks" tab in MAP: next to "All advisors". It has a wide pick and backups column, topic rotation, Upcoming, checkboxes, and Schedule selected… with a summary of channels for each advisor (the Q4 proposal). UI 3–3.5 days, backend 2.5–3.
  • C. "Weekly pick" toggle that opens a full-width panel under the row, plus a separate light Weekly picks overview page (advisor, picks due, status, Open in MAP). The separate page is shown so you can compare, but I don't recommend it. UI 2.5–3 days, backend 1.5–2.

Every option has: Why picked, fit score, Created and Updated dates, the needs-update skip with its reason, Swap with the optional Created from/to filter (the #372 pattern), and MAP's own schedule dialog (all channels) and cancel dialog. Use the top bar's Try buttons and states (Default, Loading, No picks yet, Error, Needs update, Scheduled).

Decided

  • Q3: all channels (website, social, email), exactly as MAP schedules them.
  • Q6: Cancel follows MAP and also removes the related social posts and the email draft or campaign.
  • Q2: replaced by putting the picks in MAP. MAP is admin-only, so advisors can't see their picks in this design.

Still open, with proposed defaults
