Advisor first, then compliance

For compliance advisors, the item goes to the advisor first to Approve or Decline. Only after the advisor approves does FinancialContent send the compliance approval request. If the advisor declines, compliance is never asked and reminders stop. Compliance’s Approve then auto-approves and schedules it (#415). Shown on Content approvals, Email Campaigns, and social posts.

Why the recommendation
  • No extra work for the advisor compared with today — they tap Approve or Decline once, and the hand-off to compliance just happens.
  • Decline reuses the #389 A pattern (optional reason, Undo ~10s), so advisors see one consistent Decline everywhere.
  • The 3-step tracker (Awaiting advisor → Awaiting compliance → Approved & scheduled) shows in emails, approve pages, Content approvals, and the admin list, so nobody has to ask “where is it?”.
  • B adds an optional “Note for compliance” box. Useful, but it’s an extra field most advisors will skip — easy to add later on top of A.
