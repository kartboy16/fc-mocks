Beats (recommend A)

  1. Phone-width Manage Advisor Posts — no desktop table, no horizontal scroll
  2. Search advisors by name or firm
  3. Card shows name, firm, and service count
  4. Three actions on the card: Suggest posts, Pick for me, Edit services
  5. Suggest posts → a few posts, title + short why, Use or Dismiss. Close returns to the advisor list
  6. Pick for me → one post, why this one, Use this post or See another. Not buried
  7. Edit services → add or remove chips. Done closes the sheet; the count updates
  8. After Use, the card can show “Using: {title}”

Where Suggest and Pick sit in B and C

  • B: Show actions expands the card. The three actions are inline. The chosen flow renders under them. Back to advisors collapses to the list.
  • C: Tap a row for a hub with the three actions. Suggest posts drills in and Back returns to the advisor list. Pick for me and Edit services return to the hub first.
