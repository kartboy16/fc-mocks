Second chart colour, highlights
Why a colour might change: calculators put white text on your main colour, so it has to be dark enough to read. If it's a bit light, we use a darker shade of it automatically. If it's far too light, calculators keep the standard teal. The app's default blue (#1976d2) isn't treated as a brand colour, so it's ignored.

Your main colour is also used for your article list buttons (it was the "Primary Colour" in Customize & Deploy).
