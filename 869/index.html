#869 Blocked send when the sending domain isn’t verified

Recommend A as the first slice: a hard-block dialog at the point of Send or Schedule, in both Create Campaign and the campaigns list, plus a short unverified-domain hint on the Create Campaign form so the advisor sees the problem before they click. The send is blocked. There is no “use default sender” option. Two actions: primary Verify your domain opens Email Campaigns → Settings; secondary Get help setting up your domain opens a prefilled message to support. The campaign stays a draft, or an already-scheduled campaign keeps its existing schedule — the new schedule is not saved. B disables Send and Schedule up front, which needs a domain-status check on every load and is easier to miss on the list. C, a pre-flight checklist, is heavier than a two-action block needs.

Fixtures are Jane Advisor, Your Firm Name, and yourfirm.com only. The same hard block is shown from Send and from Schedule. Compare with Today, where a confirm offers a default sender and Create Campaign then shows a broken error.
