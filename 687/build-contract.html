#687 Rewrite in my voice: build contract

Issue: #687. Mocks: hub. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev hold until Alex picks.

Today (read-only, at 6499dd7)

  • Library posts use FC's wording, and advisors edit them by hand.
  • AI already adapts social copy: ai.adaptPostForSocial, ai.rewriteSocialPost(originalText, instruction) and ai.generateWebsiteShareCopy in server/ai-service.js. But there's no saved voice profile and no guardrail checks.
  • The disclaimer is advisor_configs.disclaimer, shown by the editor's disclaimer plugin (ory_editor/disclaimer).
  • Website posts follow the advisor's compliance mode (email, external or off, in imports/utils/complianceMode.js). The email mode sends a PDF for approval (server/website-compliance-pdf.js).

Recommendation: A, a rewrite panel in the post editor plus a Voice profile settings page

  • A voice profile (Settings › Voice) and A rewrite (Content › post › Rewrite in my voice).
  • It's a one-time setup that you edit when you like, and the rewrite opens where the post already lives. It's the fewest new screens, and the same profile can later serve email and social.
  • B: guided steps (1 Your voice, 2 Pick a post, 3 Compare, 4 Approve). It's good for the first time, but slow every time after that.
  • C: the profile as one editable "style card" with sample sentences, and the rewrite in a full-screen compare view. It's friendly and visual, but less precise to edit, and full screen is a bigger build.

In and out of scope

  • In:
  • Voice profile (build, review, edit, rebuild, tick posts in or out, paste samples).
  • "Rewrite in my voice" on library and suggested posts.
  • Side by side with locked-value highlighting, the locked-value diff check and blocking, the new-claim check (best effort), and the byte-for-byte disclaimer check.
  • Approve, which hands off to the existing compliance or schedule flow.
  • Audit trail and phone layout.
  • Out:
  • Auto-publish (never).
  • Rewriting email or social with the profile (later, but reuse the profile).
  • Bulk rewrite of many posts.
  • Writing new posts from scratch.
  • Changing the disclaimer.
  • Any wording on legal or compliance meaning (placeholders only).

Build note

  1. Voice profile extraction:
  2. Fetch the advisor's own posts (WordPress REST /wp-json/wp/v2/posts when available, otherwise crawl the Insights page links on their domain only, up to about 20 posts). Strip navigation, boilerplate and disclaimers.
  3. Exclude FC library posts the advisor has published, so we don't learn FC's voice. Match them against HTMLPosts by title or embedding.
  4. One AI call returns structured JSON: {tone[], phrases[], avoid[], avgSentenceWords, signOff, address, samples[]}. Also compute sentence length and sign-off directly, not by AI.
  5. Store it on a new voice_profiles record (advisorId, sourceUrls[], excluded[], profile, builtAt, editedAt, version). Advisor edits overwrite fields. A rebuild keeps edited fields unless reset.
  6. Need at least 3 posts, or pasted samples (at least about 300 words).
  7. Locked-value detection (before rewriting): extract from the original:
  8. Money and percentages ($7,000, 18%, 4.5%).
  9. Dates and tax years (March 1, 2027, 2026 tax year).
  10. Numbers attached to rule words (limit, deadline, maximum, minimum, age, rate, contribution, withdrawal).
  11. Account and tax terms (TFSA, RRSP, RRIF, RESP, FHSA, CPP, OAS).
  12. Named products: a list per post or category, plus capitalised fund and product names.

Wrap each value in a placeholder token (⟦L1⟧) when prompting, and tell the model to keep tokens unchanged.

Limits: - Regex and lists miss values written in words ("seven thousand dollars") and rules that are paraphrased ("you can contribute up to…" turned into "the cap is…"). - They can over-lock incidental numbers. - Detection is a guardrail, not compliance sign-off. Compliance review stays required when the advisor's mode needs it. 3. Diff check after generation: - Re-extract from the rewrite and compare it with the original's locked set. - Every original token must appear exactly once, unchanged. If any value is missing, changed or extra, the rewrite is blocked (blocked state). - "Put back" swaps the original value in and runs the check again. - The new-claim check: a second model pass, or rules (guarantee, safe, risk-free, beat, always, never, best), flags sentences with no support in the original (claim state). It's best effort, so mark it that way. - Approve stays disabled while anything is blocked. The server also re-runs the checks on approve, and never trusts the client. 4. Disclaimer: - Never send it to the model. Remove it before rewriting and re-attach the stored string afterwards. - On approve, assert sha256(rewrite.disclaimer) === sha256(advisor_configs.disclaimer at approve time), byte for byte. If it fails, block. - The "Identical to original" badge reflects this check. 5. Audit trail per rewrite, in post_rewrites: - advisorId, sourcePostId, voiceProfileVersion, model and provider, prompt hash. - Original and rewrite text, the locked set, the check results (each value, claim flags, disclaimer hash). - Each regenerate, and who approved, when (PT), and their IP. - The id handed to compliance or scheduling. - Keep the history even if the advisor rejects it. 6. No auto-publish: - Approval creates the advisor's draft copy and calls the existing path: compliance send in email mode, the external queue, or schedule when compliance is off. - No code path publishes from the rewrite screen. Add a test for that. 7. White-label: rewritten Insights posts and share links never link to FC's own domain. Reuse the existing white-label rule.

Open questions

  1. Which posts count as "theirs"? Only posts on their own domain, and should we exclude FC library posts they've published? (Suggested: yes, exclude.)
  2. Minimum to build a profile: 3 posts, or pasted samples of about 300 words?
  3. Who can approve a rewrite: the advisor only, or assistants and FTT staff acting as the advisor?
  4. In compliance email mode, should the compliance PDF show the original and the rewrite, or just the rewrite?
  5. Which values are locked? Confirm the list: money, %, dates, tax years, ages, account types, named products. Should account names like "TFSA" also be locked as words?
  6. New-claim check: block (as mocked) or only warn? Who decides what counts as a claim?
  7. "Edit it myself": should hand edits re-run every check before approve? (Suggested: yes.)
  8. Should the rewrite update when FC updates the library post (for example, a new TFSA limit)? Or flag the rewrite as "out of date", or keep it frozen?
  9. How long do we keep the audit trail, and who can see it (advisor, FTT staff, compliance)?
  10. Can the advisor rewrite the same post again later (versions), and is there a limit per month (AI cost)?
  11. Should the voice profile also be used for social and email now, or later?
