#687 Rewrite in my voice

Advisors rewrite FC library posts to sound like them. We learn their voice from their own site posts, lock numbers, dates, rules, product names and the disclaimer, and block any rewrite that changes them. The advisor approves, and the rewrite goes through the normal compliance or schedule flow. Nothing auto-publishes. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev hold until Alex picks.

Recommend A: a Voice profile page in Settings, plus "Rewrite in my voice" inside the post, with original and rewrite side by side, locked values highlighted, a blocked state and Approve. About 10.5 to 14 days.

New (Oct 29): Voice sampling setup, Alex's follow-up direction. The voice is learned only from the advisor's own writing: a site crawl that skips Insights, their links with a paste fallback, a review list, and a prompt when there are fewer than 3 samples.
