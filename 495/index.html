Use an AI answer for your website, social and email

After the AI writes a post, the advisor can turn it into a website post, a social post and/or an email campaign without retyping. They can tap a choice or just say it in their own words (“schedule the post”, “put this on my site”). Everything is saved as a draft after a Confirm step. Nothing is published or sent from the chat.

Directions

Recommended

A · Action bar + review sheet

Pick one or more destinations under the answer. One sheet with a tab per destination shows each version (social shortened, email subject suggested). One button creates all the drafts.

 Least build

B · Open each editor, prefilled

A button per destination opens today's Share dialog, social composer or Create campaign with the chat text in it. One at a time, text copied as-is.

 Alternative

C · Publish kit side panel

The AI writes all versions side by side in a panel. Edit, then create or schedule each card. Most to look at, heaviest on phone.

Typed requests (all directions)

Per Alex: advisors won't know trigger words. After any answer, the AI reads what the advisor types and decides if it's a request to create/schedule/send (show a confirm card with the destinations, a preview and any date mentioned: Confirm / Change / Not now), unclear (ask “Website, social, or email?”), or a rewrite (just rewrite). Nothing is created without Confirm. The chips/buttons stay as a visible fallback.

Why A

It's the only option that does “one or more destinations, no retyping” in a single reviewable step, and it shows exactly what the AI changed for each place before anything is saved. It reuses the server code already on playground (private website post, social scheduling, ses.createCampaign), switched to create drafts. B is cheapest but takes three trips and pastes the full article into social. C rewrites every channel up front, which means more waiting and a long scroll on phone.

Heads-up from the code

  • Playground already has a chat-first version (“Where should this go?” → When → Confirm Send). Its Confirm Send publishes and schedules for real (website, social, email send). The mock switches the default to drafts.
  • Typed intent today is a fixed phrase list (aiLandingIntent.js), which is why “schedule the post” wasn't picked up. The contract moves free text to an AI classifier.
  • The same full chat text goes to every social network today, with no shortening or character limits.
  • The #458 subject prefix isn't applied when the chat creates an email campaign.
