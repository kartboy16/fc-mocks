RRSP season is a good time to look at the bigger picture, not just the deadline.

Direction C · publish kit

One button opens a side panel where the AI writes a version for every destination at once. Edit each card, then create or schedule each one.

  • Best for seeing all versions together.
  • Every channel is rewritten, even ones the advisor didn't want. More AI calls and more waiting.
  • Heavy on phone: the panel becomes a long full-screen scroll.

This screen

One entry point instead of picking destinations first.

Writing

The kit takes several seconds: one AI rewrite per card.

Kit

Each card has its own Create draft, Rewrite and (social) Schedule. “Create all as drafts” does them together. Cards for sections the account doesn't have are hidden.

Done

Each card flips to Draft saved + Open.

Not connected

Social card becomes a Connect card. Email saves without a list.

Phone

Works, but four stacked editable cards is a lot on a small screen.

Typing works too

The chips/buttons are a visible fallback. Advisors can also just type “schedule the post” or “put this on my site”. The AI works out the destinations and date and shows a confirm card first. See the “Typed request” tabs.

Typed request

The advisor never has to know a trigger word. After any answer with a draft, the AI reads the message and decides: is this a request to create / schedule / send, a rewrite, or a question?

Here it found two destinations (LinkedIn, Email) and a date (“Tuesday morning”). It shows a confirm card. Nothing is created until Confirm. Open in publish kit opens the normal review for edits.

Other phrasings that land here: “schedule the post”, “put this on my site”, “email this to my clients”, “post it to LinkedIn Tuesday”, “make this a newsletter”.

Not sure where

The AI is confident this is a “use it” request but not where. It asks one short question with the destinations as chips. If it's unsure whether it's a request at all, it asks “Want me to set this up, or change the wording?”

Rewrite

“Add my booking link” is about the wording, so the AI just rewrites. No confirm card. The fallback chips/buttons stay under the new version.
