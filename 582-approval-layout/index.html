#582 / #595: a cleaner approval-email section in Create/Edit Mailchimp Campaign

Designer-first mock. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev hold until Alex picks. This is only the layout of the dialog section; the #595 email/confirm-page work is unchanged.

The problem today: the "Send approval email" checkbox sits inline with a full-width "Send approval email on" date field and a long helper, then a comma-separated "Approval Recipients" box. It's cluttered and easy to read the time as the audience send date.

Recommend A. One card with two plain choices ("As soon as the Mailchimp draft is ready" or "At a date and time I pick (Pacific)"), email chips, and a yellow line that says this is only the approval email. Nothing is hidden behind an Edit click, it reads top to bottom right under the draft-only banner, and it's the smallest build (about 1–1.5 dev days).

Directions

  • A. Grouped "Send approval email" card with radio choices and chips (recommended)
  • B. Compact one-line summary ("Approval email to 2 people · as soon as ready · Edit") with a sub-panel
  • C. Two lanes: Audience send vs Approval email, with a "What happens" strip

Each has tabs for Unticked, Ticked (as soon as ready), Ticked (set time), Error (bad email + past time) and Mobile 375px. All are clickable: tick, switch, type or paste emails, try a duplicate, then Save to pipeline.

Build

  • Build contract (.md): field mapping to today's saved fields (no schema change), states, chip rules, validation, copy, effort, open questions.

Open questions

  1. 15-minute steps for the time, or any minute?
  2. Editing after the approval email already went out: show it read-only, or allow re-sending?
  3. Keep prefilling recipients from Settings → "Send test emails to"?
  4. Should the server reject a past time? Today it silently sends right away.
