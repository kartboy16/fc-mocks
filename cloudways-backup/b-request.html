What’s in a backup of example-advisor.ca?

✓ Included: your WordPress site

  • Pages and posts
  • Your news page, including posts FC published to it
  • Media library (images, PDFs, files)
  • Plugins and themes
  • WordPress settings and users
  • This site’s database

Technically: the site’s web folders (public_html and private_html) and its database.

✗ Not included: things kept in FC

  • Your FC content library and post history
  • Email contacts, lists and campaigns
  • Social media posts and connections
  • Your FC account and settings
  • Your domain name and email mailboxes (these stay with your domain provider)

FC keeps these separately. Backing up or restoring your website doesn’t change them.
