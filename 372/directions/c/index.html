Direction C notes: The tidiest row. A dashed + Created date chip sits next to Post history. It opens a small popover with presets and From/To fields. Once applied, it becomes a blue removable chip in the same style as the include chips. Validation lives inside the popover, so nothing changes until Apply.

Trade-off: one extra click to open, and the applied chip can get lost when an advisor list already has 30+ exclusion chips. That is why it sits in the toolbar, not in the chip strip.
