Build contract · Manage Advisor Posts (Direction A)

Full markdown: build-contract.md · Mock: locked Direction A · Hub

One-liner

The Posts cell is a compact, independently scrollable past-post list (~108px) stacked above short horizontal suggestion cards, inside a soft ~300px cell height. Schedule stays visible, and two advisor rows can peek below the filter bar.

Locked layout

  • Keep Past and Suggested visible together; no mode switch or past popover.
  • Stack Past above Suggested with a thin divider.
  • Use a soft Posts-cell max-height of approximately 300px (implementation target ~280–320px) so content does not make the table row grow without bound.
  • Target two advisor rows peeking on a typical laptop viewport below the filter bar.

Past list

  • Label it Past · N (or the existing equivalent with count).
  • Each compact row has a thumbnail/platform icon, title, date, and channel.
  • Independent vertical scroll, targeted at ~108px (acceptable ~96–120px); extra history must not grow the cell.
  • Use one compact, truncated title line where needed. Empty state: No past posts (or equivalent).

Suggested cards

  • Horizontal carousel; cards do not wrap into another row.
  • Short image region of ~72–88px (mock: ~80px).
  • Title appears once in the body—no title-on-image plus duplicate.
  • One-line excerpt and 1–2 tags.
  • Schedule and Skip remain visible on every card without hover or expansion.
  • Empty state: No suggested posts (or equivalent), without reserving a tall blank carousel.

Acceptance criteria

  • ☐ Compact scrollable Past list above short horizontal suggestion cards.
  • ☐ Past rows expose icon, title, date, and channel; scroll is independent.
  • ☐ Cards use ~72–88px image, title once, one-line excerpt, 1–2 tags, and visible Schedule/Skip.
  • ☐ Posts cell has soft ~300px height discipline and no unbounded row growth.
  • ☐ Crowded, scroll-past, schedule, skip, empty-past, and empty-suggestions states work.
  • ☐ Existing page chrome and non-Posts columns remain unchanged.

In scope

  • AdminSuggestPostCard and the past-post list (or successors), including sizing, overflow, empty states, and Schedule/Skip presentation.
