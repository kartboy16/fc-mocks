Compliance approves like advisors do — and that’s the final OK

For advisors whose compliance team must sign off, let compliance tap Approve the same way advisors do (email button or in-app). That Approve flips the item to Approved & scheduled in FinancialContent automatically — no second approve step on our side. Decline is there too and stops reminders. Shown for email campaigns and social posts.
