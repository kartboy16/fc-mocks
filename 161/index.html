FC Ads · Phase 1

Ads Phase 1 · visual directions (#161)

A Canada-only paid ads workspace for Google Search + location and Meta Lead Ads, covering campaign creation, approval, funding, FC-hosted lead capture, and performance.

A

Console Dense

Ads lives inside the FC app shell as a dense section: a campaign table first, a right-side wizard drawer over the list, a separate admin approval queue, and performance tabs on campaign detail.

Best when: advisors and CS already live in the console and want power-user density without leaving list context.

Open board →
B

Guided Wizard-First

Ads home is a softer card grid with a hero “Create campaign” CTA that opens a full-page stepped wizard with a progress rail. Approval uses a large creative-preview modal, while performance gets a dedicated big-KPI dashboard.

Best when: many advisors are new to paid ads and need the clearest guided create path.

Open board →
C

Split Ops

Role-aware top tabs separate Advisor workspace from Approver queue; the wizard pairs left-rail steps with a live landing/ad preview. Funding is its own nav item and performance embeds mini charts in expandable rows.

Best when: the same product must serve creators and approvers without context-switching apps.
