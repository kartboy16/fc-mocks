#601 AI website builder with built-in compliance checks

Problem: advisors can't build or change their own website inside FC, so they use outside AI site builders that have no compliance check, no FTT hosting and no support contact.

The advisor describes the site in a chat on the left, and the assistant builds it in a live preview on the right. The chat, preview and publish flow are the same in every direction; only where the compliance checker shows up changes. The checker mirrors the real My posts checker (#964, on staging): the same rules, wording, states and send gate. Every page has a Desktop / 390 toggle, or add &phone=1 to any link. All data is fictional (Jane Advisor, Alex Client, Your Firm Name, example-advisor.ca).

Not agent-ready. #601 is still an idea, and its plan calls for a hand-run hosting pilot first. Compliance rule wording and disclaimers are placeholders until Legal supplies them. The real checker has no testimonial rule; the testimonial quote in the mock is flagged by the Superlative claim and Past performance rules.

Recommendation: A, the Checks panel under the preview. It matches the checker UI already approved for My posts, reuses the most code, and keeps findings visible while the advisor chats.

Directions

A. Checks panel under the preview Recommended

A Checks panel stays docked under the preview, with a status bar above it ("Checks 3 to review: 2 compliance, 1 proofreading."). Highlights in the preview; Show in preview jumps to them. It is the same panel, chip and cards as the approved My posts checker, so it has the most reuse and findings never scroll away. The trade-off is a shorter preview, though the panel can collapse to its bar.

B. Checks tab in the right pane

Preview and Checks are tabs, and the Checks tab has a badge (3, ✓, ! or a spinner). A toast appears after each edit, and the Checks tab opens automatically at Publish. The preview stays full height, but findings are one click away and easy to miss.

C. Checks posted in the chat

The assistant posts each check result as its own message, with finding cards in the thread, and highlights show in the preview. It feels the most conversational, but older results scroll away, and it reuses the least of the existing checker UI.
