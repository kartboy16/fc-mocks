Replace this file?

The link will change. Anywhere you pasted the old link (a website, an email signature) will show a broken image until you paste the new one.

Open decision: should replacing keep the same link instead? That's what most people expect, but it's a bigger change. Today FC gives a replaced headshot a new link and removes the old file.

Delete this file?

Its link will stop working. Anywhere it was pasted will show a broken image. Posts and emails already sent are not changed.
