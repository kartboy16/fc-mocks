#908 Mailchimp subscriber management in FC

Recommend A as the first slice: a Mailchimp Subscribers tab with a paged table and a side drawer for add, edit, and change history. The page is clearly labelled as the Mailchimp audience, not the FTT Mail list. Add requires a CASL consent checkbox and a consent source. Every row has visible Unsubscribe (or Re-subscribe) and Remove buttons; Permanently delete stays under ⋮ and in the drawer. There is also bulk select with Unsubscribe and Remove. Re-subscribing someone who unsubscribed shows that Mailchimp will send a confirmation email and leave them Pending. B puts the same actions in modals and adds a list-wide History tab, which is useful for an audit sweep but splits the per-subscriber work. C is a compact card list with a detail page — better if most advisors work on a phone, but more routing than this needs first. A already works at 390 with a full-screen drawer.

Fixtures are Jane Advisor, Your Firm Name, subscriber@example.com, and example.com only. This view is only for advisors still on Mailchimp. There is no Mailchimp ↔ FTT Mail sync. Compare with Today, where Mailchimp Audiences is read-only and there is no Subscribers tab. CASL wording in the add form is a placeholder for compliance review.
