Ask (Angelina / Atlantic Wealth–Sabrina, Tue Oct 6): Advisors need a Decline option in Content approvals. Ignoring a piece keeps reminder-to-approve emails coming. Decline should stop those reminders, leave Approve unchanged, and show declined status to FTT admin. Related: #124 (not a duplicate).
Soft Dev hold · Designer-first · not agent-ready. Alex / Angelina have not locked A, B, or C. Do not wake Soft Dev. Do not mark agent-ready.
Why A
  • Stops reminders safely with a light confirm, so an accidental tap doesn’t bury a piece.
  • Optional quick-pick reason (Not relevant / Timing / Compliance / Other) helps FTT without blocking advisors who just want reminders to stop.
  • Soft Undo for ~10 seconds plus an Approve later link into Content approvals keeps a way back without a heavy undo forever.
  • B is faster but gives FTT no insight and no undo. C requires a reason every time, which is heavier for advisors who only want the emails to stop.
Open questions for Alex / Angelina
  1. Confirm step? All three mocks include at least a light confirm. Keep it?
  2. Optional vs required reason? A = optional quick picks (+ short free text for Other). B = none. C = required. Which?
  3. Undo / approve later? A offers Undo ~10s + “Approve later”. B/C have no undo (C can still approve later from the list if you re-open). Keep soft undo?
  4. Notify FTT? Mock assumes status-only in admin (Declined badge + optional reason tooltip). Email/Slack notify only if you ask.
  5. Suggest Posts / Pick for Me avoidance? Out of scope for v1 unless you say yes — noted on the contract. Decline does not change Suggest / Pick in these mocks.
