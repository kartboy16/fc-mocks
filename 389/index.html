Why A is still the pick
  • Fits “no clicks” best: decline, then one tap on a suggestion, and you’re done. No second Approve screen.
  • The ~10s Undo is what makes one-tap approval safe: a wrong tap undoes the decline and the swap together.
  • Optional reason helps FTT without slowing advisors who just want the reminders to stop.
  • B keeps the approval gate strictest but adds a step and brings back reminders for the new pick. C’s required reason adds friction before the swap. Pick B if “choosing = approving” is a compliance concern.
