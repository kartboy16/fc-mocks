#599 Weekly picks review: build contract

Status: Designer mock only. Not agent-ready. Soft Dev UI hold until Alex picks. The phase 1 backend (profiles, history, topic tagging) can go ahead in parallel, as Alex's comment allows.

Rev 2 (Oct 20): this revision adds content freshness from Alex's comment 6295711214: created and updated dates, a needs-update state, and a created-date filter on Swap. See Rev 2 changes below.

Mocks: Hub · A (recommended) · B · C

Rules the UI must make clear (all directions)

  • Nothing is scheduled until it's approved. Approving hands the post to the normal scheduling flow.
  • Drafts never publish on their own. There is no auto-schedule and no auto-publish.
  • Slates are built every Monday at 5:00 AM PT. Profiles re-sync nightly at 2:00 AM PT.
  • Posts flagged as needing an update are skipped until they're refreshed (Rev 2).
  • These appear as pills at the top of the queue and the slate, and again in the toast after Approve or Skip.

Open question (do not build around a guess)

Alex's spec has an unfinished sentence: "and then provide within the". It may be about where or how the slate is delivered.

The mocks show an orange Open question callout on the queue ("Delivery of the review queue") and under the slate ("What happens after Approve"). Anything that depends on it, such as delivery by email or Slack, a digest, or showing the slate to advisors, stays out of scope until Alex answers.

Rev 2 changes (content freshness)

  1. Dates on every pick and every Swap option: "Created Mar 4, 2025 · Updated Sep 12, 2026". If the post hasn't been edited, it says "Not updated since" in place of the Updated date. These also show on the queue's top pick and on posts that were skipped for needing an update.
  2. Needs update. There are two cases:
  3. Skipped at build: the post was flagged before Monday's 5:00 AM PT build, so it's never picked.
    • The slate shows a "Skipped at build: needs update (N)" panel. For each post: title, topic, dates, and "⚠ Needs update: {reason}. Flagged {flaggedAt} by {flaggedBy}." Sample reasons follow the comment: "Outdated figures: TFSA contribution limit", "Outdated figures: CPP/OAS rates", "Outdated figures: tax brackets". Flagged by Content bot or an admin.
    • Copy: "These would have fit {advisor}, but they're flagged as out of date, so the engine skipped them. They come back once the post is refreshed."
  4. Flagged since build (mock assumption, see Q8): a picked post gets flagged mid-week, for example by Content bot's periodic review.
    • The pick shows "Flagged since build: needs update", its title is struck through, and the reason box says "It can't be approved until the post is refreshed. Swap it, or leave it skipped."
    • Only Swap is offered. Approve all leaves it out.
    • To see it, use the new Needs update button in the state bar.
  5. Queue:
    • A new "Posts skipped: needs update" counter (A, B and C). It's clickable and filters the list.
    • A row tag: "N skipped at build: needs update" and/or "N flagged since build: needs update".
    • A new warning filter option: "Posts skipped: needs update".
    • A new rule pill: "Posts that need an update are skipped".
  6. Swap:
    • Flagged posts are never in the ranked list.
    • A link, "Show N posts hidden because they need an update", lists them greyed out and unselectable, with their reasons.
  7. Created-date filter on Swap and search. This reuses the #372 Direction A design: two compact floating-label date fields, "Created from" and "to", next to the search box, with a small Clear link.
  8. Empty means no limit. Each field works alone (on or after, or on or before), and dates are inclusive.
  9. Future dates are blocked (max is today).
  10. Status line: "Showing posts created Jan 1, 2025 – Dec 31, 2026 · 5 of 7 options".
  11. Empty result: "No options created … Widen the dates or clear the Created filter."
  12. If "to" is before "from", the field turns red with a plain message: "The "to" date is before "Created from". Pick a later date. Still showing the last range that worked." The last range that worked stays applied.
  13. #372 isn't locked yet. Alex's comment says to reuse it "if Alex locks it", so this follows #372's recommended A, and Rev 2 changes if #372 lands differently.
  14. Freshness down-rank: older posts score lower but aren't excluded. The fit score already includes this. The Swap explainer still says what's left out, and the dates make the ranking visible.
  15. Open question unchanged: the new comment doesn't answer "and then provide within the", so the callouts stay.

Screens

  1. Review queue (Weekly picks review)
  2. The week line shows: "Week of Mon Oct 19 · Built Mon 5:00 AM PT · Profiles re-synced 2:00 AM PT".
  3. Status counters (Pending review, Partly approved, Approved, Skipped, With warnings). In A they're clickable and act as filters.
  4. Filters: search advisors, status, this month's topic, and warnings (Has a warning, Thin fit, Streak override, Missing products/services).
  5. Each row shows: advisor name and firm, warning chips, this month's topic, the top pick (thumbnail, title, one-line reason, and "+ N more picks, 2 backups each"), chips for the last 3 months' topics, the status with "x of y approved", and Review.
  6. Bulk actions:
    • Approve selected. This leaves out rows with warnings, and the UI says so.
    • Skip selected this week.
    • Rebuild selected.
    • Approve all with no warnings.
    • Rebuild all slates.
  7. Advisor slate
  8. The header shows: name, firm, this month's topic, the last 3 months' topics, warnings, and the slate status.
  9. A help box for each warning type. "Missing products/services" links to the existing Services panel.
  10. Each pick shows: thumbnail, title, topic chip, the item's status, the fit score, "Why this pick:" with the reason, and its backups.
  11. Each pick has Approve / Swap / Skip. Undo appears once a pick is approved or skipped.
  12. Slate actions: Approve all (covers the remaining pending and swapped picks), Skip this week, and Rebuild this slate.
  13. Swap
  14. Backup 1 and Backup 2 come first under their own heading, then the other options ranked by fit (or search results).
  15. Each one shows: rank, thumbnail, title, topic chip, reason, and fit score.
  16. The 3rd-month-in-a-row warning is shown on the alternative it applies to.
  17. Search by title or topic. "No posts match" appears when nothing is found.
  18. Explainer: "Posts already on this advisor's site, or used in the last 12 months, are left out."
  19. Confirm swap is disabled until a post is chosen. Cancel closes without changes.
  20. A swapped pick shows "Swapped, needs approval". It is never approved automatically.
