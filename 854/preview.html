What the latest federal budget could mean for your RRSP and TFSA

4 min read · Updated Nov 4, 2026

This is sample article copy for the mock. It is not tax advice.

The latest federal budget included several proposals that may affect how Canadians use registered accounts. The details can change before anything becomes law, and what matters for you depends on your own plan.

For RRSPs, the questions to ask are familiar: how much contribution room you have, whether a contribution this year is the right move, and how withdrawals might be taxed later.

For TFSAs, the budget may affect contribution limits or withdrawals. A TFSA can still be a flexible place for savings you may need before retirement.

If you have questions about what the proposals could mean for you, this is a good time to review your year-end plan.
