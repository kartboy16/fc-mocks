#854 Suggest one library post to one advisor by email

Recommend A — an admin-only Send suggestion item in the Content Library card's existing Share menu that opens a drawer to pick one advisor. The usual trigger is a timely post the admin just found, so the post stays in context. B is better when the admin is already in Manage Advisor Posts looking at one advisor. C adds a new compose entry point without the context of either surface. All three share the same email and result pages.

Sample post: What the latest federal budget could mean for your RRSP and TFSA. Fixtures are generic (Jane Advisor, Your Firm Name, yourfirm.example, example.com). Nothing publishes until the advisor approves.
Entry-point revision (Direction A / PR #855): The dedicated Send suggestion button is removed. Admins open Share on the library card and choose Send suggestion (Admin only badge). Non-admins see the same Share menu without that item. The drawer is unchanged: Search advisors, Advisor source include/exclude filter, Social and Email on by default and editable, website Insights required. Share menu items match the live ShareDestinationPicker and the #824 D mock, plus the new item.
