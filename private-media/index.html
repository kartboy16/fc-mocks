Decisions (all seven closed, Alex, Nov 19, 10:55 PM PT)

Alex accepted every proposed default.

  1. Support and admin upload or delete in an advisor's My media? [Decided: no. View only, as mocked.]
  2. The limits. [Decided: JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB, at least 600 × 315 px, up to 200 images per advisor.]
  3. Deleting an image that a post uses. [Decided: allowed, with the warning as mocked. A scheduled post goes Held.]
  4. A live post whose image is deleted. [Decided: it loses the image on the website too. Emails already sent can't be recalled.]
  5. Body picture button. [Decided: it does not pick from My media in this build.]
  6. My media and the account. [Decided: deleted with the account.]
  7. Alt text. [Decided: none for now.]

Picked: Direction A plus the My media page (Nov 19, 10:55 PM PT). B and C not picked. Software developer holds the build, handed over by Chief of Staff.
